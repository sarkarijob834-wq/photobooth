Privacy Policy

Your photos stay in your browser.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Summary

PhotoboothOnline (photoboothonline.xyz) is designed to collect as little information as possible. The photobooth runs in your browser and your pictures stay on your device.

Camera And Photos

When you press Start camera, your browser asks for permission. The live video is shown only on your screen. Photos and strips are created in your browser’s memory and are not uploaded, stored or shared by us. You can stop the camera at any time, and closing the tab discards any strip you did not download.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names or email addresses through the photobooth. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time) for security and reliability. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

Cookies And Analytics

The site does not currently set advertising cookies. If we add analytics or advertising in future, we will update this page and explain your choices before doing so.

Search Tools And Third Parties

We use Google Search Console to understand how the site appears in search results. Outbound links to other sites are governed by their own policies.

Children

The site is general-audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise camera use by young people.

Your Rights

Because we hold almost no personal data, there is little to delete, but you may contact us at contact@photoboothonline.xyz about any request relating to information you have sent us.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
