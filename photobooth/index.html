What The Photo Strip Maker Does

This photo strip maker turns four quick webcam pictures into the classic vertical photobooth strip, then lets you restyle it before you save. Choose a four-photo strip, a shorter three-photo strip, a 2×2 grid or a single portrait, add a frame colour and caption, and switch between six filters without retaking anything. The finished image is a high-resolution PNG that works for social posts, invitations, scrapbooks and prints.

Photo Strip Maker FAQ

Is this online photobooth free?

Yes. The photobooth on this page is completely free. There is no account, no watermark, no trial period and no download limit.

Are my photos uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. The camera feed, the photos and the finished strip are all processed inside your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers, so when you close the tab the photos are gone unless you downloaded them.

How do I download my photo strip?

After the last countdown the finished strip appears under the camera. Press “Download PNG” to save it. On a phone, the file lands in your downloads or you can long-press the image to save it to your gallery.

Why can't the photobooth access my camera?

Most often the permission was blocked or another app is using the camera. Click the lock icon beside the address bar, allow the camera for this site, close video-call apps and reload the page.
