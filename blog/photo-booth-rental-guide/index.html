Photo Booth Rental Guide: What To Ask, What It Costs And How To Choose

By PhotoboothOnline · Updated October 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Hiring a booth can make an event feel special, but quotes vary widely and the details matter. This photo booth rental guide explains what affects the price, which questions to ask and how to compare providers with confidence.

What Affects The Price Of A Photo Booth Rental

Prices differ by country, season and provider, so instead of quoting numbers, it is more useful to understand the factors that move the price. Expect cost to rise with the length of hire, the type of booth, the number of printed copies, the level of customisation, travel distance, and whether an attendant is included. Weekend evenings in peak season usually cost more than weekday afternoons, and setup in difficult venues, such as upper floors without lifts, may add fees.

Choose The Type Of Booth

The main options are an enclosed classic booth, an open-air booth, a mirror booth and a 360 or video booth. Open-air booths suit large groups and big spaces, enclosed booths are cosy and traditional, mirror booths look elegant, and 360 booths create dramatic videos. Questions To Ask Before You Book

  • How long is the booth staffed, and can the time be extended?
  • Are unlimited prints included, or do I pay per print?
  • Is there a digital gallery, and how long will it stay online?
  • What is the backup plan if equipment fails?
  • Can the overlay, backdrop and props be customised?
  • How much space, power and setup time do you need?
  • Do you carry public liability insurance?
  • What is the deposit and cancellation policy?

Check Quality, Not Just Price

Ask for sample images taken in real events, not only marketing photos. Look at lighting, sharpness and how groups appear in the frame. Read recent reviews on independent sites and ask for references from similar events. A cheap booth with poor lighting can disappoint, whereas a slightly more expensive booth with a reliable attendant may deliver far better value.

Plan The Space And Timing

Measure the location and confirm doors, stairs and ceiling height. Make sure there is a power outlet within a safe distance and a spot for guests to queue. Schedule the booth for the time when guests are relaxed, such as after dinner or during the cocktail hour, and make sure the organiser or venue knows when the supplier will arrive.

Read The Contract

The contract should state the date, hours, location, equipment, prints, digital delivery, setup and breakdown times, deposit, cancellation terms and what happens if the booth cannot operate. Never rely on verbal promises, and keep a copy of the quote. If anything is unclear, ask before signing, and check applicable local consumer rules.

Cheaper Alternatives

If a rental is outside your budget, you can build a DIY photobooth or use the free online photobooth on a laptop or tablet. These do not include staff or instant prints, but they provide the same fun at almost no cost. You can also hire a booth for a shorter period, such as two hours during the busiest part of the event.

Rental Checklist

  1. Decide your budget and guest count.
  2. Choose the booth style and backdrop.
  3. Request quotes from several providers.
  4. Compare inclusions, not only price.
  5. Check samples, reviews and insurance.
  6. Confirm space, power and timing with the venue.
  7. Sign a clear contract and pay the deposit.
  8. Confirm all details a week before the event.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a photo booth rental cost?

It varies by location, booth type and hire length. Compare quotes from several providers and check what is included, such as prints, attendants and digital galleries.

How far in advance should I book a photo booth?

Popular dates fill early, so book as soon as your date and venue are confirmed, especially for weddings and holiday events.

Is an online photobooth a good alternative to a rental?

For small, casual events, yes. For large events that need instant prints and staff, a professional rental is a better fit.
