About PhotoboothOnline

PhotoboothOnline is an independent website built around one simple idea: everyone should be able to enjoy a photobooth, whether or not there is a booth nearby. The site combines a free online photobooth that works in any modern browser with original guides to photobooth history, booth types, party ideas and photo tips.

What We Publish

Our guides are written in plain language and aim to be practical. We cover where the photobooth came from, how different kinds of booths compare, how to plan a booth for a wedding or birthday, and how to take better strips with the equipment you already own. Historical details are general facts that can be confirmed on reference sites.

How The Photobooth Works

The booth runs entirely in your browser using your device camera. Photos are assembled on your screen and are never uploaded to our servers. Read the privacy policy for details.

Independence

PhotoboothOnline is not affiliated with any photobooth manufacturer, rental company or camera brand. If you have a correction or a suggestion, please contact us.
